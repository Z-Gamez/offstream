Privacy policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

This policy covers ClipForge, the editing and upload tool used by the Offstream channel ("we"). ClipForge is used only by Offstream's owner, to manage Offstream's own accounts.

What ClipForge accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with the account owner's permission, ClipForge uses the YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload, to upload videos to the Offstream channel, and youtube.readonly, to read the channel's name to confirm the right channel is connected.
  • Instagram (Meta): with the account owner's permission, ClipForge uses the Instagram Graph API to publish Reels to the Offstream Instagram account and read its username.

How it's stored

Sign-in tokens are kept only on the owner's computer, encrypted with Windows' built-in data protection. ClipForge has no servers of its own and keeps no copy of this data anywhere else.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share data from Google or Meta APIs with anyone. Videos and captions are sent only to YouTube and Instagram, to be posted on Offstream's own accounts.

ClipForge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google and YouTube

ClipForge uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions or Meta's Business integrations settings. Disconnecting in ClipForge also deletes the stored tokens. See Data deletion for step-by-step instructions.

Contact

Questions: message @offstream_tv on Instagram.
