About ClipForge

ClipForge is Offstream's private editing tool. It runs on the channel owner's own computer. It helps cut clips the channel is permitted to post, adds captions, and uploads the finished Shorts and Reels to Offstream's own accounts through the official YouTube Data API and Instagram Graph API.

ClipForge is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

Clips are posted with the creators' permission or through their official clipping campaigns. To ask about a clip, contact us on Instagram at @offstream_tv.
