Data deletion

Last updated October 24, 2026

ClipForge is a private tool used only by Offstream's owner. It keeps no user data on any server. The only data it holds is the sign-in tokens for Offstream's own YouTube and Instagram accounts, stored encrypted on the owner's computer.

How to remove ClipForge's access and delete its data

  1. Instagram / Facebook: go to Facebook Settings → Business integrations, find ClipForge and click Remove. This revokes access immediately.
  2. YouTube / Google: go to Google account → Third-party apps & services, find ClipForge and choose Delete all connections.
  3. In ClipForge: Setup → Connect accounts → Disconnect. This deletes the stored tokens from the computer.

Videos already posted stay on YouTube and Instagram until they're deleted there, like any other post.

Request deletion

If you believe ClipForge holds data about you and want it deleted, message @offstream_tv on Instagram with "data deletion" and we'll confirm within 30 days.
